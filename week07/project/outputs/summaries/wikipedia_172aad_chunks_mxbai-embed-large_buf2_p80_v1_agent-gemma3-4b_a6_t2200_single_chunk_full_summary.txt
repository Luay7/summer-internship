Saudi Arabia, officially the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA), is a country in West Asia located within the center of the Middle East, covering much of the Arabian Peninsula with an area of approximately 2,150,000 km2. Bordering the Red Sea to the west and numerous other nations including Jordan, Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Yemen, and the Gulf states to the east and north, it is separated from Egypt and Israel by the Gulf of Aqaba. The country’s terrain is predominantly arid desert, with lowland, steppe, and mountainous regions. Riyadh serves as its capital and largest city, alongside Jeddah and the two holiest cities in Islam: Mecca and Medina, home to a population of 33.7 million.

Pre-Islamic Arabia, encompassing modern Saudi territory, has been historically significant, featuring early human activity and serving as the birthplace of Islam under the leadership of Prophet Muhammad in the seventh century. Following his death, Islamic expansion led to the creation of caliphates – Rashidun, Umayyad, Abbasid, and Fatimid – which extended Muslim rule across North Africa, Central Asia, South Asia, and Iberia.

Founded in 1932 by King Abdulaziz, uniting various regions under the House of Saud, Saudi Arabia is currently an absolute monarchy defined as a sovereign Arab Islamic state with Islam as its official religion and Arabic as its official language. Historically dominated by the Wahhabi movement, the country has undergone social reforms since the 2000s. As a major global player, Saudi Arabia is a key U.S. non-NATO ally, possesses substantial oil reserves (second largest globally), controls significant gas reserves, and holds the world’s 19th-largest economy by nominal GDP and 17th largest by PPP. It's recognized as a high-income economy within the World Bank, offers free education and healthcare, and has a diverse population with approximately 40% comprised of foreign workers. Saudi Arabia is an active member of numerous international organizations including the Gulf Cooperation Council, United Nations, Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, Arab League, OPEC, and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation.
